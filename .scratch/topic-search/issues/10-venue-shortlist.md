## Question

Which 3–5 conferences or workshops fit a solo, empirical evaluation paper on LLM security-alert triage vs cheap baselines and risk-based alerting (see [Choose the problem](06-choose-the-problem.md)), with a submission deadline between about 2026-11-08 and early 2027? Prefer mid-tier or regional security, AI-for-security, or applied-ML venues and workshops (e.g. ones co-located with ACSAC, AsiaCCS, ESORICS, DIMVA, or AI/ML conferences), with arXiv as the fallback. For each, give the deadline, page limit and format, review model (single- or double-blind), whether workshop papers are archival, and fees, and say whether the page limit should shape the brief's scope. Check each fact on the venue's official call for papers.

Findings go in `notes/venue-shortlist.md`. Non-blocking: the brief can be drafted without it.

## Answer

**Shortlist** (from the research agent's note; facts were taken from official pages on 2026-10-08, but I have not re-checked them myself, so open the chosen venue's call for papers before relying on a date):

1. **ACM CODASPY 2027:** abstract 2026-11-16, paper 2026-11-23, notification 2027-01-25; 12 pages ACM sigconf, anonymized; no dual submission. The 23 Nov date is tight against the 8 Nov draft target.
2. **ACM AsiaCCS 2027, cycle 2:** due 2026-12-11, notification 2027-03-31; 12 pages plus references, double-blind; at least one author must register at the full rate. The call says primarily-ML submissions are "not encouraged".
3. **AI-SEC 2027** (a workshop of AINA-2027, not AAAI): due 2026-12-10, notification 2027-01-10; 10 pages in Springer LNCS style, Scopus-indexed; a registered author must present. Review model and fee not stated (an estimated EUR 400).
4. **WOSOC at NDSS 2027** (best topic fit; **no 2027 call yet**, deadline estimated late December 2026 from last year): 4–8 pages.
5. **DIMVA 2027** (backup; no 2027 call yet; estimated December 2026 or mid-February 2027): 25 LNCS pages, in-person presentation required.

**arXiv fallback:** a first submission needs an endorsement. **Scope suggestion for the brief:** plan an 8–10 page paper with A1 as the core and A2 as an extension; that fits AI-SEC, trims to WOSOC and expands to the 12-page venues. Submit to one archival venue at a time. Registration is required at most venues and an ACM open-access charge may apply; whether the author's institution covers it is unchecked. Not verified by the agent: the WOSOC, DIMVA and ESORICS 2027 calls, and AI-SEC's review model and fee.
